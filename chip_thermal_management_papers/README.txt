Chip thermal-management literature, 2018–2026

82 records: 30 from 2018, 21 from 2019, and 31 from 2020–2026.
38 full-text PDFs downloaded and verified; 44 records have no retrievable PDF in this collection.

The 2018–2019 additions were screened for chip, IC, package, or processor thermal modeling/management, including HotSpot, Ansys Icepak, Siemens FloTHERM, and work citing the original Temperature-Aware Microarchitecture paper.
HotSpot can reproduce some architecture-level thermal models. Fluid-flow and detailed package results may require a different solver or simplified boundary conditions.
